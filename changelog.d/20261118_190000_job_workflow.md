### Added

- `POST /api/jobs/{id}/split` divides a job in the annotation stage between several people,
  and the job menu offers "Split between people"
- A Null button in the annotation screen tags the frame with the job's `null` label and moves on
- An Approve button on the review screen accepts the job and sends frames with open issues back
  to the annotator (<https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat/pull/XXXX>)

### Fixed

- Images of a job whose frames were split or sent back no longer fail to load from stale cached chunks
  (<https://github.com/cvat-ai/cvat/pull/XXXX>)
