### Added

- Jobs keep an annotator and a reviewer. `GET /api/jobs/people?ids=` returns them for
  several jobs and `PATCH /api/jobs/{id}/people` changes them, handing the job to the new
  person when it is with them
- `POST /api/jobs/{id}/send_to_review` moves an annotated job to review and gives it to
  the reviewer. The job's annotator can do this, as well as task staff
- The board shows each job's annotator and reviewer, with a menu to change them, and
  remembers the last project chosen
- A Send to Review button on the annotation screen

### Changed

- Frames sent back on approval go to the job's annotator by default
